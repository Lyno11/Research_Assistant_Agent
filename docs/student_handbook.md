# Northfield University Student Handbook (Sample)

This is a fictional document written for workshop practice.

## Graduation requirements

Undergraduate students must complete 120 credits to graduate. At least 30 of those credits must be earned at Northfield University, and at least 40 must be in courses numbered 300 or above.

Students must hold a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher, and a GPA of 2.5 or higher in their major courses. Every student completes a capstone project in the final year.

Students apply for graduation through the Registrar by October 1 for spring graduation and by March 1 for fall graduation.

## Academic standing

A student whose cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 is placed on academic probation for one semester. Students on probation may enroll in no more than 13 credits and must meet with an academic advisor twice during the semester.

## Course withdrawal

Students may withdraw from a course without a grade penalty until the end of week 9. A withdrawal after week 9 requires approval from the Dean and is recorded as WF.

## Academic integrity

Submitting work produced by an AI tool without disclosure is treated as unauthorized assistance. Instructors state in each syllabus which uses of AI tools are permitted. A first violation results in a zero on the assignment and a report to the Office of Academic Integrity.
