# Northfield University Career Center Guide (Sample)

This is a fictional document written for workshop practice.

## Internship timeline

Most large technology employers open summer internship applications between August and October of the previous year. The Career Center recommends having a reviewed resume ready by September 1.

## Resume guidance

Keep a student resume to one page. List projects with the problem, the tools used, and a measurable result. Include a link to a code repository for technical roles.

## Talking about AI projects

Recruiters ask how a project was tested, not only how it was built. Be ready to explain what data the system used, how you checked that its answers were correct, and where a person stays in control of decisions.

## Appointments

Students book 30-minute advising appointments through the Career Center portal. Drop-in hours run Tuesday and Thursday from 1 pm to 3 pm in Hale Hall room 104.

## Career fairs

The fall career fair is held in the third week of September and the spring fair in the second week of February. Students must register in the portal at least two days before a fair.
